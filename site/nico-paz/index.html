01

The short version

United have taken up the extra year on Bruno Fernandes's contract, so if he goes next summer there's a fee, and The Athletic reports Paz is on the list of options. This piece isn't Bruno against Paz. It's about whether Paz is the real thing on his own terms, and two seasons of data say he is.

He lives in the pocket

Right of centre, just inside their half, where he can open up on his left foot. Same spot both seasons.

He does both jobs

Takes men on like a winger, tackles like a holding midfielder. Nobody in his position in Serie A combines the two over a full season.

The edges are raw

Too many shots from range and he doesn't win it high. Both are the stuff a 22-year-old works on.

Every claim below is either a two-season trait (2025/26: 2,882 league minutes; 2026/27: 857 so far) or is labelled as a single-season number.

02

Where he lives

Fàbregas calls him "an 8 or a 10", and in practice he roams. But the touch maps show a clear home: he drifts into the pocket right of centre, just inside the opposition half. Left-footed and on his inverted side, once he turns he's facing the whole pitch with every option open. He also drops into his own half to collect when Como are building.

03

You know it's coming

His move: pick it up in that pocket on the right, shift it inside onto the left foot, and either bend it towards the far post or slip it through for a runner. Everyone in the ground knows it's coming.

What the tape shows

05

He does both jobs

This is the one that makes him different. Most 10s either beat a man or win the ball. Paz does both, and he did it over a full season.

  • Only Messias sits near him, and he did it in roughly 670 minutes against Paz's 2,882. Miretti matches his tackling in about 610 minutes but takes nobody on.
  • It has held up this season: 2.73 tackles and 2.10 dribbles per 90 in 2026/27, against 2.84 and 2.15 last season. That's a trait, not a hot streak.
  • Possession makes the tackling stand out more. Como have more of the ball than almost anyone in Serie A, so their players get fewer chances to defend. Adjusting for that would push his number up, not down.

How he shoots

He shoots on sight. A lot of it is from range, and that drags his chance quality down, but when he hits the target he hits it clean. His shots on target were worth more than the chances they came from in both seasons, and his free kicks are a weapon.

09

What's still raw

The honest bit

Nobody's the finished article at 22. These are the parts to watch.

  • Shot choice. 60% of last season's shots came from outside the box at about 0.06 xG each. It's improving, but it's the clearest thing to coach.
  • He doesn't win it high. He was 10th of 22 attacking midfielders for winning the ball in the final third (0.8 per 90, both seasons). He tracks back and nicks it deeper rather than pressing from the front.
  • The final ball is good, not elite. 13 big chances created last season is a strong number, but FotMob's traits put him in the bottom half (36th percentile) for chances created among Serie A attacking midfielders and wingers this season.
  • Serie A is not the Premier League. Numbers usually come down a little after a move to a tougher, faster league, and this season's sample is only about ten games.
  • The price and the paperwork. Real Madrid hold a buy-back option for 2027/28 (reported at around 80 million euros), so any deal goes through them as well as Como.
10

A 10 for a decade

He's 22, already a starter in a Champions League side (33 league starts last season), and the bits he's elite at have held for two seasons. Get him in now and that's your 10 for the best part of ten years.

Method and sources

  • Player data: FotMob season pages for Serie A 2025/26 and 2026/27 (to 27 Nov): touches, shots, xG, xGOT, tackles, dribbles, recoveries. Touch and shot maps are drawn from FotMob's location data.
  • Positional comparison: FotMob league stats for Serie A 2025/26 filtered to attacking midfielders (22 qualifying players): tackles, dribbles, ball won in the final third.
  • The two-season rule: something only counts as a trait if it shows up at the same level in both seasons. Ten-game swings (his box touches doubled this season) are treated as noise.
  • Same league, same position: percentiles and rankings only compare Paz with Serie A attacking midfielders. We don't line up Serie A numbers against Premier League ones.
  • What the stats count: "duels won" is left out because it double-counts tackles and dribbles. Tackle counts depend on how often a team is without the ball, so possession is discussed alongside them.
  • Role and style: Hudl StatsBomb on his inverted role; Como's 2026/27 shape.
  • News: Bruno's contract option and United's interest in Paz as reported by The Athletic, 27 Nov 2026. Real Madrid's buy-back: club statement, June 2026; price as reported.
  • Video: the broadcast first half of Como v United (21 Oct 2026), cut to his involvements, checked frame by frame against the match clock. Paz identified by his number and, where shown, the name on his shirt.
  • What we didn't have: full-match video, tracking data and possession-value models (OBV, xT). The "You know it's coming" board is an illustration of a pattern seen in his maps, not a reconstruction of one goal.
